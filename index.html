How to Play

  1. Tap a bolt to lift its top nut — matching nuts stacked together lift as a group.
  2. Tap another bolt to place them. Nuts only land on a matching color or an empty bolt.
  3. Sort every bolt so each one holds a single color, top to bottom.
  4. Fewer moves = more stars. Beat the par for 3 stars. Undo is always free.
